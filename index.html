What happens next

  1. Establish your writer identity

    Join a performing rights society as a songwriter — this gives you an IPI number — and open an account at The MLC under the same legal name. Then ask both to consolidate the records already carrying your name: CHRIS WYCHE at The MLC and WYCHE DECHRISTOPHER (NS) at BMI. Send us the IPI once it is issued, and tell us whether you have ever signed a publishing, administration or distribution agreement that covers your songwriting.

    BMI — join as a songwriterASCAP — join as a songwriterThe MLC — member portal

  2. Register Violence first, then the rest of your catalog

    Violence carries 2,424,694 measured plays and no composition record. Confirm whether anyone else wrote or produced on it, then register it with its ISRC attached at filing. After that, register the remaining songs largest first — Bloodas, Loose Screws, 4th Quarter and Love U More lead the list. Solo songs can move quickly; collaborations wait for step 04.

  3. Settle F.S.C. and Intro

    F.S.C. is registered to Antonio Robinson alone while your label credits you as composer. We need whatever exists in writing about who wrote it — a split sheet, a producer agreement, a beat licence, or even a text or DM where it was agreed. Anything in writing helps. Intro is a separate, simpler item: your recording is attached to an unrelated live work and needs a delink review before your own Intro is registered.

  4. Document the splits on your collaborations

    Wit Me (Lang Amaru, Marco Boer, Stefan Roberts) and Take A Mile (St. Wallace, Kyle Spratt) already carry your name; the missing piece is agreed splits. The same applies to your songs with Overspace, Gritt / Martys’ Smith, Kwajo, Marcus Cain, Xiamara Jennings, Kanise and Jefe Sué. A song only moves to ready-to-file when its evidence is complete. We will never file a claim on your behalf without documentation; that protects you as much as it protects us.

  5. Clean up delivery, then review the master side

    Ask Twenty20 Records (or your distributor) to deliver every release with your legal name as composer and, once you have one, your publisher — and to put Layover In Charlotte on Spotify and the Spotify-only releases on Deezer. Ask Deezer to move the 2016 F.S.C. upload off the unrelated “Volar” profile. Then review SoundExchange registration for your recordings with Twenty20 Records.
